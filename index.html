02 / How Relay works

Catch the failure. Hand off cleanly. Resume safely.

Relay sits between your workflow runtime and your alerting layer. It doesn't replace your orchestrator. It makes it operable by people who aren't engineers.

STEP 01

Intercept

Timeouts, low-confidence outputs, semantic mismatches, infinite loops. Relay catches the failure before damage spreads downstream.

STEP 02

Preserve

Inputs, outputs, intermediate steps, agent memory, API responses — frozen at the moment of failure. No blind retries, no duplicate writes.

STEP 03

Package

Operator gets a plain-English summary: what ran, what didn't, whether retry is safe, and the recommended next action. No stack traces.

STEP 04

Resume

One click in Slack. Relay restarts from the exact failure point with full audit trail. Engineering is not on call for routine recoveries.
